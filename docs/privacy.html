Unified Drive Assistant

Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026 · Silcore Engineering, Australia

This policy explains what information the Unified Drive Assistant iOS app (“the app”) uses, why, and your choices. The app is made by Silcore Engineering (“we”, “us”). We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

In short
  • You don't need an account to use the app.
  • We run no servers. Most of what the app does stays on your device.
  • We only receive information when you choose to send it to us: an expert request or a review by email.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no third-party analytics, and we never sell your information.

1. Information the app uses

Optional sign-in (Apple or Google)

If you choose to sign in, Apple or Google gives the app your name, email address (or an Apple “Hide My Email” relay address), and an account identifier. These are stored only on your device, in the iOS Keychain. They reach us only if you send an expert request while signed in, so our engineers know who they're helping. You can sign out or delete your account at any time in Settings → Account.

Expert requests (Pro subscribers)

When you send an “Ask a human expert” request, your email app sends us:

  • what you type: drive brand and model, fault code, urgency, problem description, and the optional site name and call-back phone number;
  • any photos you choose to attach;
  • your name, email and account identifier if you're signed in, and your App Store subscription ID (so we can confirm Pro access);
  • your app version, iOS version and device type (to help with troubleshooting);
  • the email address you send from.

We use this only to answer your request and to follow up with you about it.

Reviews and feedback

If you send a review from Settings → Feedback, we receive your message and the email address you send it from. We use it to improve the app and to reply to you.

Purchases

Pro subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app receives only the subscription status and a transaction ID from Apple.

2. Information that never leaves your device

FeatureWhat happens
LocationUsed to tag saved site visits and, only if you turn on Background site alerts, to notify you when you arrive at one of your own saved sites. Location is stored on your device and never sent to us.
Camera & photosUsed on-device for nameplate scanning and speed measurement. Images are not uploaded. The only exception is photos you choose to attach to an expert request.
Motion sensorsAccelerometer readings for vibration analysis are processed on your device only.
Face ID / Touch IDHandled entirely by iOS. The app only learns whether unlocking succeeded, and never receives biometric data.
Settings & historyYour preferences, saved site visits and fault history are kept on your device.

3. Optional AI guidance

The AI assistant is off by default. If you turn it on and enter your own API key for Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT) or Google (Gemini), the app sends the details of the fault you're viewing (brand, drive family, fault code, title, cause and remedy) directly from your device to that provider. It sends no personal information. That provider's own privacy policy and terms apply, and usage is billed to your account with them. Your API key is stored only in your device's Keychain and is never sent to us.

4. Who else handles information

We don't sell, rent or trade personal information. It is handled by these services only as needed to run the features you use:

  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases, iCloud Mail if you use it.
  • Google: Google Sign-In, if you choose it. Our support inbox is also hosted on Gmail, so expert requests and reviews you email us are stored there.
  • Your email provider: it delivers the emails you send us.
  • AI providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google), only if you turn on AI guidance with your own key.

Some of these services store data outside Australia, including in the United States. When we use them, we take reasonable steps to make sure your information is handled consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles.

5. How long we keep it

We keep expert requests and feedback emails for as long as we need them to help you and keep a record of the support we provided. We then delete them, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. Information stored on your device stays there until you delete it, sign out, delete your account, or remove the app.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Use the app without an account. Signing in is optional.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your sign-in data from your device and disconnects Google if you used it. To remove Sign in with Apple completely, go to iPhone Settings → [your name] → Sign in with Apple.
  • Access, correct or delete what you've sent us by emailing us (see below). We'll reply within 30 days.
  • Control permissions (location, camera, photos, notifications) at any time in iPhone Settings → Unified Drive Assistant.
  • Cancel Pro in iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.

7. Security

Sign-in details and API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain, and all network connections the app makes use encryption (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold.

8. Children

The app is a professional tool for people who work on industrial equipment. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above. If the changes are significant, we'll also tell you in the app.

10. Contact and complaints

Questions, requests or complaints: Silcore.engineering@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.
