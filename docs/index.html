Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Fault lookup, the tools and Pro all work without signing in. You can sign in with Apple or Google in Settings → Account if you'd like your expert requests linked to you.

How do I restore my Pro subscription on a new device?

Open Settings → Pro → Restore purchases while signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I cancel Pro?

In the app, go to Settings → Pro → Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Account → Delete account. To fully remove Sign in with Apple, also go to iPhone Settings → [your name] → Sign in with Apple. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription.

How does the AI guidance work?

It's optional and off by default. Turn it on in Settings and enter your own API key from Anthropic, OpenAI or Google. Only the fault's details are sent to the provider you choose.

How accurate are the vibration and speed tools?

They give indicative readings using your phone's sensors. They're useful for a quick check on site, but they don't replace calibrated instruments.
